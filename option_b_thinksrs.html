Model Q100

OBN Flagship Power: <60 mW
1PPS Disciplined Oscillator with Sub-Millisecond 60-Day Holdover
Request Datasheet / RFQ

The TimeCubic Model Q100 is an ultra-low power 1PPS disciplined oscillator module engineered specifically for battery-powered autonomous Ocean Bottom Nodes (OBN), seismic recording stations, and remote GNSS-denied sensor systems.

Consuming less than 60 mW under steady-state operating conditions, the Q100 achieves holdover stability of < ±1 ms over a 60-day mission profile without requiring external timing corrections. This performance eliminates the heavy power draw of traditional OCXOs (300 mW to 1.5 W), extending subsea mission life by 300% or cutting lithium battery pack size.

During pre-deployment operations on the vessel deck, the Q100 disciplines its internal crystal to an external GNSS 1PPS reference with phase measurement resolution down to ≤ 1 ns RMS. Time-of-Day (ToD) and phase log telemetry are accessible via RS232 serial communications.

Application Note TC-AN-01

Blind Holdover Precision in Deep-Water Ocean Bottom Nodes (OBN)

Author: TimeCubic Advanced Physics Group • Woburn, MA

In deep-sea 3D/4D seismic acquisition, thousands of autonomous Ocean Bottom Nodes are placed on the seafloor at depths up to 3,000 meters for 60 to 180+ days. Radio frequency signals from GPS/GNSS satellites cannot penetrate seawater; consequently, each node operates completely unsynchronized throughout the recording window.

Because acoustic wave travel time forms the basis of subsurface reflection mapping ($\Delta x = v \times \Delta t$), timing inaccuracies introduce serious spatial blurring and distortion in seismic imaging. For high-resolution exploration, cumulative clock drift must remain strictly within ±1.0 millisecond over the entire 60-day window.

Conventional OCXOs:
Power: 300 mW to 1,500 mW
Demands massive lithium battery packs. Limits ROV basket capacity and inflates vessel charter costs.
TimeCubic Q100 Solution:
Power: < 60 mW (70%+ Reduction)
Enables 300% longer subsea missions with verified holdover <±1.0 ms across 60 days.

Before deployment, the Q100 disciplines to shipboard GNSS 1PPS down to 1 ns RMS phase resolution. Upon node retrieval, post-mission clock drift is verified via serial telemetry for sub-millisecond seismic trace correction.
