Company Overview

About TimeCubic Inc.

Dedicated to providing ultra-low power yet high-performance timing solutions to meet today's most critical demands on SWaP-C across industries worldwide.

Over 30 Years of Frequency & Timing Heritage

TimeCubic Inc. was founded in 2017 in Massachusetts (now headquartered in North Andover, MA) as a Research and Development entity dedicated to solving the fundamental trade-off between power consumption and precision holdover in frequency standards.

Founded by a team of accomplished engineers and physicists with over 30 years of collective experience in the frequency control industry, TimeCubic holds proprietary intellectual property in low-power thermal design, precision ovenization, and advanced digital signal processing.

Our Mission on SWaP-C

Every product we develop is guided by the SWaP-C paradigm: Size, Weight, Power, and Cost. In modern applications—such as seabed ocean bottom nodes that operate unassisted for months thousands of meters below sea level—power efficiency directly translates to operational viability and mission success.

Engineering & Operations FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About TimeCubic

Key facts on our technology heritage, US domestic engineering laboratory in Massachusetts, and custom OEM manufacturing capabilities.

Where is TimeCubic located and where is engineering support performed?
TimeCubic Inc. is headquartered in North Andover, Massachusetts (USA). Our R&D laboratory, field application engineers (FAE), and environmental validation testing chambers are located locally in MA. This provides direct US Eastern Time zone collaboration, quick technical reviews, and rapid prototype turnarounds for North American and allied aerospace, defense, and seismic clients.
What is TimeCubic's core technological innovation?
Traditional OCXOs consume 1.5 W to 3 W because heating the bulk package loses thermal energy to the ambient environment. TimeCubic invented a proprietary high-vacuum micro-ovenized thermal isolation architecture that maintains SC-cut crystal resonance at sub-60 mW steady-state power (<45 mW in Model Q100). This delivers sub-ppb frequency holdover with atomic clock performance at 1/10th the power consumption.
Does TimeCubic offer custom frequencies, pinouts, and custom testing?
Yes. In addition to standard standard outputs (10 MHz, 12.8 MHz, 19.2 MHz, 20 MHz, 25 MHz, 100 MHz, 1PPS), our North Andover engineering team provides custom output frequencies (5 MHz to 100 MHz), drop-in pinout adaptations for legacy form factors (e.g. 20x20mm, 25x25mm, 14-pin DIP), and specialized screening (MIL-STD-202 vibration/shock, extended subsea screening, and custom thermal cycle calibrations).
How does TimeCubic support OEM product evaluation?
We stock engineering evaluation kits (EVKs) featuring USB/SMA interfaces, integrated power monitoring, and diagnostic software GUI. Qualified OEM programs can request loaner evaluation units with direct engineering consultation on layout, power decoupling, and vibration isolation.

TimeCubic Inc. designs and manufactures industry-leading ultra-low power frequency oscillators, disciplined timing modules, and atomic frequency standards engineered for the most demanding SWaP-C applications worldwide.
