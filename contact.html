Procurement & Custom Inquiries

Direct Engineering Engagement FAQ

How to engage our North Andover, MA technical team for custom specs, evaluation hardware, and production volumes.

What is the typical response turnaround time for technical RFQs?
All inquiries submitted through this RFQ portal or direct email (inquiry@timecubic.com) are routed directly to our Massachusetts engineering and sales desk. You will receive an engineering acknowledgement and initial spec confirmation within 24 business hours.
Can we request sample units or Evaluation Kits (EVKs) for lab testing?
Yes. Select "Evaluation Unit / Sample" under Inquiry Type in the form above. We supply pre-calibrated evaluation boards with SMA RF outputs, 1PPS sync test points, USB-UART monitoring interfaces, and real-time power logging tools to accelerate your design cycle.
Can TimeCubic build drop-in replacements for discontinued or legacy oscillators?
Yes. Many defense and subsea systems use older legacy OCXOs or atomic clocks that consume 2W to 5W or have suffered obsolescence. Our engineering team routinely designs drop-in daughterboards and custom pin-compatible packages matching your exact mounting footprint, supply voltage (3.3V or 5.0V), and logic levels while slashing operational power consumption by up to 90%.
What are typical production lead times?
Evaluation kits and engineering samples of standard frequencies (10 MHz, 1PPS) typically ship within 1–2 weeks from our Massachusetts facility. Production batch lead times range from 6 to 10 weeks depending on custom screening requirements (extended thermal cycles, low-G certification, burn-in aging).
