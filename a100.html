Quantum Precision Meets Subsea Battery Life

The Model A100 is TimeCubic's breakthrough atomic frequency standard designed to overcome the power consumption limits of legacy rubidium and vapor-cell standards. Operating under 150 mW, the A100 brings true atomic holdover to battery-operated autonomous subsea platforms and portable defense payloads.
